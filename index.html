RangeCraft

How it works
  1. Pick a species.
  2. Draw your range. Compare it with the official outline, which is view-only.
  3. Propose. Your range is scored later: new GBIF records landing inside it count for you, records landing outside count against it.

Experts are recognised by their public record (iNaturalist research-grade observations and IDs, publications), not by points here. Planning aid, not ground truth.

    RangeCraft complements official range maps. Experts propose edits; the agreement map shows where they converge and where the edge is contested.

    How ranges are scored

    A range is scored by whether new GBIF records (observed after the edit) land inside it, compared with what chance would give for its area. Drawing the whole world earns nothing.
